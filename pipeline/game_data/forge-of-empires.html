    Forge of Empires
    1. Games

    Game Description

    Please make sure your adblocker is disabled or you won't be able to play this game.

    Forge of Empires is a truly epic MMO game. Do you think Empires are negotiated over biscuits and tea? Fool! The world around you was forged in the flames of time and against the cold iron of experience. All empires started as an idea which was brought forth into the world and made real by hard work, determination, and strategies of their founders. In Forge of Empires: the game, you will start as a small stone age settlement and slowly build up an empire throughout the ever-changing tides of history. Go from stone to bronze to steam to industrial to information and beyond! This game ain't like dusting crops or growing a garden, it is a strategic massively multiplayer online role-playing game where you must learn to manage your resources in order to grow your settlement and eventually dominate the future itself!

    Category & Tags

    MMOStrategy

    Instructions

    No Empire happens by accident. It requires generations of planning, struggle, sacrifice, work, and pain. In Forge of Empires, you will use your mouse to click on the in-game menus in order to purchase and upgrade the available units and resources for that time period. As time passes your available options will also change and evolve. Your ability to manage resources and plan strategically will be imperative in eventually taking over the world!
