    Game Description

    Sniper Team is a puzzling, first person shooting game. Gather your squad and defend the objective against your enemies in this first-person action game. In Sniper Team, it’s up to you and your three squad mates to defend the objective from being destroyed. You’re fixed in a specific location, and you can’t move. But that’s why there are four of you. Enemies will come from multiple directions, and you’ll have to switch between the members of your squad to take care of all the enemies. Defeat them all, and you move on to the next mission for an even more challenging scenario. Be on the lookout for more difficult enemies, as well as supply drops that can give you a cash boost. Defeating enemies also grants you experience and money. The experience levels you up, unlocking new items that you can buy for your squad members.

    Instructions

    This is a first-person action game, and like most of them, you aim with by moving the mouse, and you fire with the left mouse button. Switch items with Q, reload with R, switch between your squad mates with 1-4, and space zooms down the scope if you have one equipped.
