    Game Description

    Stick War is a free desktop strategy game.   This is the ultimate base defense game online. Prove that you are the greatest Stick King in the land by defeating all of your stick opponents. In this real time strategy game, defend the statue of your king while simultaneously trying to destroy the statue of your enemy. Build miners to harvest gold, and then use that gold to purchase units. Those units will be your front-line defense against the enemy, and your means of destroying the statue. Progress through multiple levels, unlocking additional units along the way, until proving to the stick world that you are the one true Stick King.

    Instructions

    As Stick King, your duties include clicking buttons at the top of the screen to manage your stick army. That’s where you buy new units, or command the units you already have to defend, attack, or garrison inside your base, safe from the enemies. After completing each mission, you earn upgrade points that you can use to improve your various units, as well as your statue itself. One great tip is to defend for as long as possible, building up a sufficient army. Then, after your opponent sends their stick army to you, send yours and clear them out!
