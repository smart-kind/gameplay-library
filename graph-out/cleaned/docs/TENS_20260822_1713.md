# TENS!

- **类型**: 益智解谜
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS+Android)
- **开发商**: Kwalee Ltd
- **首次发布**: 2017
- **一句话描述**: Get ready for the ultimate challenge! Journey through diverse levels, work towards objectives that keep you engaged and entertained as you strategically place dice to make ten

## 玩法规则

Get ready for the ultimate challenge! Journey through diverse levels, work towards objectives that keep you engaged and entertained as you strategically place dice to make ten. Crate blocks and obstacles won’t make this easy so use special tiles including bombs, and powerful boosters to clear the board.

Use your intellect and efficiency to make combos, unlock new boosters, and beat high scores!  The satisfying feeling of clearing a board makes Tens! the ultimate puzzle adventure to challenge your mind.

Are you prepared to work smarter, not harder and give your IQ a boost?

Features:

Dynamic Gameplay: Match numbered tiles strategically to reach the sum of ten, each level offers a new twist through objectives.

Diverse Levels: Explore a variety of stages with various challenges and objectives to keep you engaged.

Special Tiles & Boosters: Encounter unique tiles such as bombs and utilise useful abilities to enhance your strategy.

Exciting challenges: Face off against tricky obstacles like blocker tiles, mastering strategic tile placement.

游戏的核心操作基于触摸屏交互，玩家通过单指点击、长按或滑动来控制游戏元素。每个关卡或回合都有特定的胜利条件，玩家需要在规定时间内或有限次数内达成目标。游戏采用渐进式难度设计，初期关卡用于教学引导，后续关卡逐步增加复杂度和挑战性。

## 核心循环

益智解谜核心操作 → 获得分数/通关 → 解锁新关卡或获得游戏内奖励

## 核心机制

- **触控操作**：通过点击、滑动或拖拽完成核心交互，操作简洁但需要精准时机把握
- **渐进难度**：从简单教学关卡开始，逐步引入新元素和复杂规则
- **即时反馈**：每次操作都有视觉和音效反馈，增强操作确认感

## 为什么好玩

简洁的操作配合精心设计的关卡节奏，让玩家在"差一点就过关"的驱动下不断重试。单局时间短，非常适合碎片化娱乐场景。

## 粘性来源

主要来自"差一点就成功"的不甘心感和关卡解锁的收集欲。每关都有明确目标，通过后有强烈的成就感反馈，促使玩家继续挑战下一关。

## Meta 系统

无。游戏专注于核心玩法体验，没有复杂的外围成长系统。
