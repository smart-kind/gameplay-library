## 玩法规则

PushMaster is an exhilarating hyper-casual game where you launch people into oncoming vehicles and watch them fly!

Experience the thrilling mix of excitement and guilty pleasure as you perform outrageous acts you'd never dare do in real life. Our ragdoll characters' wobbly movements add a fun twist, while the ASMR-like satisfaction keeps you coming back for more. From subways and forest railways to highways and piers, explore a variety of captivating locations.

Enjoy this easy-to-play game during your spare time and revel in the refreshing sensation it provides. However, remember never to imitate these actions in real life!
