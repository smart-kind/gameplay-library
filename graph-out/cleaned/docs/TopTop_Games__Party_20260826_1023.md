## Gameplay Rules

The app serves as a hub containing dozens of casual games within a single interface. Players browse through a grid or list of available mini games, each with a thumbnail preview and brief description.

Players tap to select a game and are taken to that game's dedicated screen. Each mini game has its own controls and mechanics - some are simple tap games, others use drag or swipe controls. The platform handles the transition between games seamlessly.

Games span multiple genres including puzzle, arcade, racing, sports, and party games. New games are periodically added to the collection. The app tracks play history and may feature recommended or trending games.

Social features may include playing with friends, competing on leaderboards, or sharing scores. Some games within the platform support local multiplayer where multiple players share one device.

## Core Loop

Browse game collection -> Play selected game -> Return to browse more

## Core Mechanics

- Game Hub System: Centralized interface aggregating multiple games into one app. Each game loads as a self-contained module within the platform wrapper.
- Discovery and Recommendation: Games are organized by category (puzzle, arcade, party) with featured and trending sections to help players find new favorites.
- Cross-Game Progression: Some platforms track overall play time, total games played, or achievement badges across all contained games.

## Why It Is Fun

The convenience of having dozens of games in one app eliminates the need to download and manage separate apps. Variety means players never run out of options.

## Retention Drivers

The constantly expanding game library keeps players returning to try new additions. Social features like leaderboards and multiplayer modes create community engagement. The low commitment per game (just tap and play) reduces the barrier to trying something new.
