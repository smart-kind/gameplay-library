## 玩法规则

The War rages on! Forge your empire with Fire and Blood like the House of the Dragon. Raise your dragon, call knights to your army, and conquer the Seven Kingdoms in this immersive strategy building game! Experience the official Game of Thrones and House of the Dragon strategy building game set in a timeless Westeros at war. Great houses clash, dragons rule the sky, and the army of the dead threatens the realm. Build your kingdom, forge strategic alliances, and battle to secure Seats of Power for your empire. Train your dragon, command your troops, and collect your favorite Game of Thrones and House of the Dragon characters as you engage in epic war and strategy battles to conquer the Seven Kingdoms! If you enjoy immersive battle strategy games, kingdom building, forming powerful allegiances, or raising dragons, Game of Thrones: Conquest is for you! GAME OF THRONES: CONQUEST FEATURES COLLECT HEROES: - Over 100 heroes from House of the Dragon and Game of Thrones await your command, including Rhaenyra Targaryen, Jon Snow and unique dragon Heroes like Syrax. - Level them up to boost your kingdom’s battle and building stats. - Use strategy and deploy heroes into battle to reinforce your armies and win the war! STRATEGY & KINGDOM BUILDING: - This strategy building game starts with you leading a house in Westeros. - Build your castle’s defenses and train knights and other troops to raise your army for war. - Research crucial technologies for battle and economy at the Maester’s Towe
