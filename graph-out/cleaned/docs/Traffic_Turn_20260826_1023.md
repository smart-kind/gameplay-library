# Traffic Turn

- **Type**: Traffic and Driving Puzzle
- **Platform**: Mobile (iOS)
- **Developer**: Voodoo
- **First Released**: 2019
- **One-liner**: A driving and traffic management puzzle game

## Gameplay Rules

Players navigate vehicles through traffic puzzles, requiring spatial reasoning and planning. Each level presents a traffic scenario that must be solved by moving vehicles in the correct order.

Vehicles can only move along their designated direction. Players must carefully plan moves to avoid blocking paths.

The goal is to clear a path or reach a target destination. Levels increase in complexity with more vehicles and tighter spaces.

## Core Loop

Move vehicles -> Clear path -> Complete level -> Unlock next

## Core Mechanics

- Vehicle Movement: Direction-restricted movement on a grid.
- Spatial Reasoning: Multi-step planning required.
- Star Rating: Based on moves used vs optimal.

## Why It Is Fun

The spatial puzzle challenge of finding the right sequence in a constrained environment.

## Retention Drivers

Each level is a new puzzle to solve. The almost-got-it feeling drives replay. Daily levels provide ongoing content.

## Meta Systems

Level progression with star ratings.

## Implementation Difficulty

Low - Grid movement and collision detection. Challenge: level design.
