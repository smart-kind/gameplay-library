No Timer. No Pressure. Just Pure Logic.

Shape Escape: Block Puzzle is the ultimate game for fans of brain teasers, logic puzzles, and strategic thinking.

Slide, fit, and escape the grid in this addictive puzzle adventure. With no timers and no pressure, every move is a calm, satisfying challenge for your brain.

- Escape the grid to unlock the next challenge
