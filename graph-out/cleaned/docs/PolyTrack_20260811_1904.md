## 玩法规则

PolyTrack is a classic car racing game with a slight twist. You race a car you've customized, on a track you might have designed yourself. This is a fast-paced, low-poly driving game featuring thrilling loops, jumps, and high-speed action where every millisecond counts.

Inspired by TrackMania, it challenges you to race against the clock, constantly striving to improve your time on various tracks built by you and the PolyTrack community! It doesn't get any more hands-on than that.We enjoyed creating our own courses and taking turns to see who could get the fastest times. Being able to use jumps and physics to find unintended shortcuts was the funniest moment: when a carefully designed course was suddenly beaten in a few seconds. If you go wrong, you can restart almost straight away, which was a nice touch as well. We all loved it, even the youngest family members. Review by FamilyGamingDatabase.How to Play PolyTrackYou can race on the racecourses you've designed, but it's probably easier to start with the existing ones. You not only have to think about beating your own record when it comes to time, but you also need to master the craft of building for speed and precision.Start by creating your track using the game’s design tools or choosing from pre-made tracks. Customize the track with various obstacles, ramps, and curves to suit your preferences. Don't know exactly what track to build? Get help from the PolyTrack community. There are plenty of forums that share design codes and customization tips.With a built-in level editor, you can unleash your creativity by designing custom tracks. You can also export and share these tracks with others for endless racing (and design) fun.The game will require time and patience, as you don't just go into it like other driving games, with your foot on the pedal and ready to rev and drift. Initially, the easiest tracks to build are straight lines, but as soon as you get the hang of it, you will have a track jumping over mountains, sending your car and score soaring.You can also customize your vehicle by selecting primary and secondary colors, adjusting the car frame and rim colors, and personalizing the rims, paint patterns, and exhaust pipes.A series of premade tracks is available for those who want to begin practicing and getting a feel for other designs.PolyTrack Unique FeaturesWhen selecting a track, you can see the best lap times recorded by verified players on it and measure yourself against them.One cool feature that differentiates PolyTrack from other driving games is the opportunity to challenge opponents asynchronously, by selecting them from the leaderboard before starting the game, and seeing them as “ghosts” as you race the track. You can also challenge yourself by shaving off some decimals from your best time and then have your car “ghosted” on the track.Available Tracks7 official summer tracks5 official winter tracks5 official desert tracksAn ever expanding collection of community tracksThe driving experience remains unaltered, independent of the track season: what changes is the scenery surrounding the track.More Games Like ThisOnce you've mastered building your tracks, you can enjoy these other thrilling driving games: Moto X3M, where you experience high-octane motocross action with challenging courses, stunts, and exciting levels, or Drift Hunters, where you test your drifting skills in this game that features a variety of cars and tracks designed for perfecting those thrilling drifts.Release DateDecember 2022PlatformWeb browser (desktop and mobile)

### 详细操作说明

游戏界面简洁直观，主要操作区域占据屏幕大部分空间。玩家需要：

1. **观察当前状态**：注意屏幕上显示的游戏元素、得分、剩余时间或生命值
2. **做出决策**：根据当前形势选择最佳操作时机和方式
3. **执行操作**：通过点击、滑动或按键来完成游戏动作
4. **获得反馈**：操作成功或失败都有明确的视觉和声音提示
5. **持续调整**：根据游戏节奏的变化不断调整策略

### 游戏流程

游戏开始时，系统会生成初始的游戏场景。玩家进入游戏后：

- **前期**：熟悉基本操作和游戏规则，这个阶段节奏较慢
- **中期**：游戏节奏逐渐加快，需要更快的反应速度和更精准的判断
- **后期**：难度达到顶峰，只有最专注的玩家才能突破
- **结算**：游戏结束后显示得分、排名等信息，并提供重新开始选项

### 得分机制

游戏通过以下方式进行计分：

- 完成基本操作获得基础分数
- 连续成功操作会触发连击加成
- 特殊操作或高难度动作有额外分数奖励
- 部分游戏内道具可以倍增得分
