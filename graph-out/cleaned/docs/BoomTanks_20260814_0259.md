## 玩法规则

3vs3 A great war of tanks with quick and real multi-play! You can enjoy an individual game or a team game in a variety of mode. Tanks with different special capabilities are prepared! Make stronger tanks through upgrade and vigilance! *Mode* +1 vs 1 : A mode to engage in man-to-man battle, dominate an opponent! + 3 vs 3 : It is a competition by teams with 3 to 3! Explode opponents' tanks! + 1 vs All : It is an individual showdown! After exploding all tanks, make it to the top! ( Since a laser will come out from both sides after 30 seconds in the above 3 modes, dominate opponents quickly ) + Occupation Battle : If 3 to 3 laser control tower is occupied and then kept, a laser will remove opponents! + Basketball : 3 to 3, you will win if you get 2 goals! + Defense : 3 to 3, you will win if you explode an opponent's base! Features: + Offering items free of charge every day + No advertisement + Quick development and delightful action + Granting new capabilities through tank vigilance!! (Available from Level 10 ) + Strategic team play + Offering friendly games for playing with friends + Ranking by tank + Easy control + Offering rewards for achievement + Box item
