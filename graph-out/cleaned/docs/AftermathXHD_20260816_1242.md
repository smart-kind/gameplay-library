## 玩法规则

Players navigate through post-apocalyptic environments in a top-down or isometric action shooter. The XHD designation indicates enhanced high-definition graphics for modern mobile devices. Players control a character moving through levels filled with enemies, obstacles, and destructible elements. Combat involves shooting various enemy types while managing ammunition and health.

Players collect weapons, power-ups, and resources scattered throughout levels. The game features multiple levels with increasing difficulty and boss encounters that test all skills learned throughout the level.

Twin-stick touch controls use one virtual joystick on the left side of the screen for movement and another on the right side for aiming and shooting. The HUD displays health, armor, ammunition count, and available weapons at the bottom of the screen. A mini-map shows the level layout and enemy positions.

游戏以关卡为单位推进，每个关卡包含多个战斗遭遇和探索区域。玩家可以通过探索发现隐藏的补给和武器升级。游戏的难度曲线逐步递增，新敌人类型和武器逐步引入，让玩家有足够的学习曲线。

## 核心循环

Navigate level - Engage enemies - Collect weapons and power-ups - Defeat boss - Unlock next level - Upgrade character

## 核心机制

- Twin-Stick Shooting: Players use virtual joysticks for movement and aiming, providing precise control over character movement direction and shooting direction simultaneously.
- Destructible Environments: Walls, barriers, and cover can be destroyed by weapons fire, dynamically changing the battlefield layout and forcing players to adapt tactics.
- Weapon Variety: Multiple weapon types including pistols, shotguns, rifles, and explosives with different damage profiles, fire rates, and ammunition management requirements.
- Health and Armor System: Players manage both health and armor bars, collecting pickups to restore depleted reserves during combat. Different enemy types deal different damage profiles.
