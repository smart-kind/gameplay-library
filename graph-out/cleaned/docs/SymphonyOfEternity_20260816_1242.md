## 玩法规则

Players control a party of characters in a traditional Japanese role-playing game adventure. The game features Kemco signature JRPG formula with turn-based combat, exploration of dungeons and towns, and a narrative-driven storyline. Players explore a world map, enter towns to buy equipment and accept quests, and navigate dungeons filled with monsters and treasure.

Combat is turn-based, with players selecting commands such as attack, magic, items, and defend for each party member. Character progression involves earning experience points from battles, leveling up, and learning new abilities and spells.

Touch controls navigate menus for combat commands and exploration. A world map shows available locations, and tapping enters towns or dungeons. The game features a traditional JRPG menu system with options for items, equipment, status, and system settings.

游戏以章节为单位推进，每个章节包含多个城镇探索、地牢冒险和剧情事件。玩家可以通过支线任务和隐藏内容延长游戏时间。游戏的战斗系统深度足够支撑数十小时的游玩，角色成长系统提供持续的成就感。

## 核心循环

Explore world and dungeons - Encounter enemies - Turn-based combat - Level up characters - Progress story - Face stronger challenges

## 核心机制

- Turn-Based Combat: Classic JRPG combat where players and enemies take turns selecting actions, requiring strategic resource management of MP, items, and character positioning.
- Character Roles: Party members have distinct roles such as fighter, mage, and healer with unique skill trees and abilities to develop through leveling and equipment choices.
- Equipment and Crafting: Players collect and equip weapons, armor, and accessories to enhance character stats. Some items can be crafted or synthesized from materials found in dungeons.
- Story-Driven Progression: A narrative unfolds through dialogue, cutscenes, and key story events that gate access to new areas and content, providing motivation to continue playing.
