# Alien Space Retro

- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)

- **一句话描述**: Retro-styled space shooter arcade game with waves of alien enemies.

## 玩法规则

Players control a spaceship at the bottom of the screen, moving left and right while shooting at waves of alien enemies that approach from above. The game features classic arcade-style gameplay with retro-styled graphics reminiscent of 1980s arcade shooters. Players dodge enemy fire, collect power-ups dropped by defeated enemies, and face increasingly difficult waves of aliens.

Boss enemies appear at the end of stages, requiring pattern recognition and skillful maneuvering to defeat. Each boss has multiple attack patterns and phases, forcing players to adapt their strategy mid-fight. The game screen displays score, lives, and active power-ups in a heads-up display at the top or sides of the screen.

Touch controls allow players to drag their ship to move freely within the play area. Shooting may be automatic or triggered by a separate fire button. The game features a lives system where players start with a set number of lives and lose one each time their ship is hit by enemy fire or collides with an enemy. Extra lives may be collected as power-ups or earned by reaching score milestones.

游戏整体节奏紧凑，以波次为单位推进战斗。每清除一波敌人后会有短暂的恢复时间供玩家收集道具和调整位置。游戏的难度曲线经过精心设计，新敌人类型和攻击模式逐步引入，让玩家有足够时间学习和适应。

## 核心循环

Move ship and shoot - Dodge enemy fire - Collect power-ups - Defeat wave - Face boss - Advance to next stage

## 核心机制

- Wave-Based Enemies: Enemies appear in predetermined patterns and formations, each wave increasing in difficulty and complexity. Enemy types include basic grunts, fast scouts, heavy tanks, and special abilities.
- Power-Up Collection: Defeated enemies drop power-ups including spread shot, shield, speed boost, and bombs that temporarily enhance player capabilities. Power-ups have limited duration or a set number of uses.
- Boss Battles: Each stage culminates in a boss fight with unique attack patterns that require learning and adaptation. Bosses have multiple phases and increasing aggression as their health decreases.
- Score Chaining: Consecutive kills without taking damage multiply score, rewarding skilled play and encouraging risk-taking behavior.

## 为什么好玩

The nostalgic arcade feel combined with modern mobile controls creates an accessible yet challenging shooter experience. The escalating difficulty curve and boss patterns provide a satisfying skill progression that rewards practice and pattern recognition.

## 粘性来源

The drive to beat your high score and progress further through the stages keeps players coming back. Boss pattern mastery and the desire for perfect runs with no damage taken drive repeated play sessions.

## Meta 系统

Score-based progression with unlockable ships or weapon types. High score leaderboards and achievement system for completing specific challenges such as no-death runs or speed clears.
