How to Play Tap-Tap ShotsThe gameplay in Tap-Tap Shots is pretty straightforward: it is a one-button game where each click propels your ball. Depending on your tap, your ball may or may not make the shot.The net moves to a different height and location each time you make the shot. This will test your click skill. The game, in essence, is an endless series of rounds of practice shots. Your best scores are tallied, incentivizing you to improve upon your performance in the last round.The key to the game is to find the right rhythm when tapping, hence the name tap-tap. ### 游戏流程

游戏开始时，系统会生成初始的游戏场景。玩家进入游戏后：

- **前期**：熟悉基本操作和游戏规则，这个阶段节奏较慢
- **中期**：游戏节奏逐渐加快，需要更快的反应速度和更精准的判断
- **后期**：难度达到顶峰，只有最专注的玩家才能突破
- **结算**：游戏结束后显示得分、排名等信息，并提供重新开始选项

### 得分机制

游戏通过以下方式进行计分：

- 完成基本操作获得基础分数
- 连续成功操作会触发连击加成
- 特殊操作或高难度动作有额外分数奖励
- 部分游戏内道具可以倍增得分
