# Space Squadron

- **类型**: Strategy/Action
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)
- **开发商**: PocketGamer reviewed title
- **首次发布**: N/A (delisted)
- **一句话描述**: Space strategy game involving commanding a squadron of spacecraft.

## 玩法规则

Players command a squadron of spacecraft in space-based missions. The game involves assembling and managing a team of different spacecraft, each with unique roles and abilities. Players deploy their squadron into missions involving combat, exploration, or resource gathering. During missions, players issue commands to individual ships or the squadron as a whole, managing positioning, targeting, and special abilities.

Touch controls allow players to tap ships to select them, then tap destinations to move or tap enemies to attack. A command interface provides access to special abilities and formation controls. Players can also drag to select multiple ships for group commands.

The game screen shows the space battlefield with the player squadron, enemy forces, and mission objectives. Each ship displays its health bar, shield status, and energy levels. Mission objectives are displayed as markers on the battlefield, and a minimap shows the overall situation.

游戏以任务为单位推进，每次任务持续时间适中，适合移动端游玩。玩家可以随时暂停和保存进度。游戏通过不同的任务类型和敌人配置保持新鲜感，同时通过舰船升级系统提供长期的成长感。

## 核心循环

Assemble squadron - Deploy on mission - Command ships in real-time - Complete objectives - Earn resources - Upgrade and expand squadron

## 核心机制

- Squadron Composition: Players build a balanced squadron with different ship types including fighters, bombers, support vessels, and scouts, each with unique stats, abilities, and tactical roles.
- Real-Time Tactical Combat: Missions unfold in real-time with players issuing commands to ships, requiring quick decision-making and tactical positioning under time pressure.
- Ship Progression: Individual ships gain experience from combat and can be upgraded with better weapons, shields, and special abilities, creating attachment to specific ships.
- Mission Variety: Different mission types such as escort, assault, reconnaissance, and defense require different squadron compositions and strategies, keeping gameplay fresh.

## 为什么好玩

The satisfaction of building a well-balanced squadron and watching tactical plans unfold in real-time combat creates a compelling blend of strategy and action. Each ship feels unique and valuable to the team composition.

## 粘性来源

Collecting and upgrading different ship types creates a collection drive similar to creature collection games. The variety of mission types and increasing difficulty keeps players engaged in optimizing their squadron composition.

## Meta 系统

Ship collection and upgrade system. Squadron expansion through recruitment or mission rewards. Mission star ratings and leaderboard competition for mission completion times.

## 实现难度

Medium - Multi-unit real-time AI control and pathfinding to ensure intelligent ship behavior without overloading mobile CPU, plus efficient rendering of multiple ships and projectiles.
