# Incredible Express

- **类型**: Puzzle/Strategy
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)
- **开发商**: PocketGamer reviewed title
- **首次发布**: N/A (delisted)
- **一句话描述**: Puzzle strategy game involving building and managing train routes and delivery systems.

## 玩法规则

Players build and manage train routes across a map, connecting cities and delivering cargo or passengers. The game involves planning efficient rail networks, managing train schedules, and optimizing delivery routes. Players lay track between stations, assign trains to routes, and manage resources like money and materials.

As the game progresses, players handle more complex logistics with multiple train types, competing demands, and expanding maps. The challenge lies in building an efficient network that maximizes revenue while meeting delivery deadlines.

Touch controls let players drag to draw track segments between stations, tap to assign trains to routes, and swipe to pan and zoom on larger maps. A resource panel shows current money, materials, and available trains. The map view shows the network, stations, and active trains moving along routes.


游戏以关卡为单位推进，每个关卡有独特的地图布局和配送需求。玩家可以通过优化路线和提高效率来获得更高的评分。游戏没有复杂的内购机制，所有火车类型和地图区域通过游戏进度逐步解锁。

## 核心循环

Plan route - Lay track - Assign trains - Manage deliveries - Earn income - Expand network - Handle more complex logistics

## 核心机制

- Route Planning: Players draw or place track segments to connect stations, optimizing for distance, terrain cost, and delivery efficiency. Different terrain types affect construction cost.
- Train Scheduling: Multiple trains must be coordinated to avoid collisions and maximize throughput on shared track segments. Passing loops and switches add strategic depth.
- Resource Management: Players earn income from deliveries, spending it on new track, trains, and station upgrades. Budget management is key to sustainable growth.
- Progressive Complexity: Later levels introduce express trains, freight versus passenger differentiation, and time-sensitive deliveries that add layers of strategic planning.

## 为什么好玩

The satisfaction of building an efficient rail network and watching trains run smoothly on carefully planned routes creates a deeply rewarding puzzle-strategy experience. The gradual complexity increase keeps the challenge engaging without becoming overwhelming.

## 粘性来源

The optimization puzzle of making routes more efficient and expanding the network to cover more destinations creates a compelling drive for improvement. Time pressure on deliveries adds tension to each session.

## Meta 系统

Network expansion with unlockable regions and train types. Achievement system for efficiency milestones such as revenue targets and delivery speed records. Scenario-based levels with specific objectives.

## 实现难度

Medium - Multi-train real-time scheduling and pathfinding algorithms to avoid collisions while maximizing throughput, plus large map rendering and zoom functionality.
