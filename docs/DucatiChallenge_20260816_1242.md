# Ducati Challenge

- **类型**: Racing/Sports
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)
- **开发商**: PocketGamer reviewed title
- **首次发布**: N/A (delisted)
- **一句话描述**: Officially licensed Ducati motorcycle racing game with challenge-based gameplay.

## 玩法规则

Players control Ducati motorcycles across various racing challenges and tracks. The game features official Ducati branding and real motorcycle models from the Ducati lineup. Players navigate through race tracks, managing speed, braking, and cornering to complete time trials and races. The challenge mode presents specific objectives such as completing laps within a time limit, achieving top speeds on straights, or navigating through traffic.

Players can upgrade and customize their Ducati motorcycles for better performance. The touch controls use tilt-based steering or virtual buttons for throttle, brake, and lean control during races. Some versions may use swipe gestures for shifting gears.

The game screen shows the race track from a third-person or first-person perspective behind the motorcycle. The HUD displays current speed, lap time, position in the race, gear indicator, and a mini-map of the track. Track boundaries, opponent motorcycles, and environmental obstacles all require attention during races.


游戏以挑战赛模式为核心，每场比赛持续时间较短，适合移动端碎片化游玩。玩家可以通过不断练习提高自己的驾驶技术和对赛道的熟悉度。游戏没有复杂的内购系统，所有摩托车型号和升级通过挑战完成逐步解锁。

## 核心循环

Select Ducati bike - Choose challenge - Race against clock or opponents - Earn rewards - Upgrade bike - Tackle harder challenges

## 核心机制

- Official Ducati Licensing: Real Ducati motorcycle models with authentic handling characteristics, each bike feeling different to ride based on engine power, weight, and aerodynamics.
- Challenge-Based Progression: Instead of traditional race series, players tackle specific objectives such as time trials, speed tests, and precision challenges that focus on specific riding skills.
- Bike Customization: Players upgrade engine, suspension, brakes, and tires to improve lap times and handling characteristics, creating a meaningful progression system.
- Ghost Racing: Race against your own best time or other players ghost data for self-improvement, allowing players to see exactly where they lose time on the track.

## 为什么好玩

The authentic Ducati riding experience combined with focused challenge objectives creates bite-sized racing gameplay perfect for mobile sessions. The variety of challenges keeps the experience fresh, and the real brand licensing adds credibility.

## 粘性来源

The pursuit of better lap times and the satisfaction of mastering each challenge with progressively upgraded bikes creates a compelling progression loop. Time-limited special challenges add urgency and variety.

## Meta 系统

Challenge completion unlocks new Ducati models and tracks. Bike upgrade system with performance parts. Leaderboards for time trial records and challenge completion times.

## 实现难度

Medium - Motorcycle physics simulation realism including lean angle, tire grip, and aerodynamics calculations, plus track environment rendering at racing speeds.
