## 玩法规则

Do you think you are smart enough??? Then try and win in the ultimate 15 Puzzle Challenge! Become the ONLY master of the classic puzzle game!

Invented more than 100 years ago this simple puzzle game is also known as Gem Puzzle, Boss Puzzle, Game of Fifteen or Mystic Square.  The object of the puzzle is to place the numbered tiles in order in a 4x4 square by making sliding moves that use the empty space.

15 Puzzle Challenge is offering you great chance to refresh your skills in the well-known puzzle game. All of us used to play it once…but everything has changed since then. With 15 Puzzle Challenge you can play on your iPhone or iPad and compete against anyone around the globe. Use Game Center to challenge your friends or random players. The competition is really tough and without the practice you won’t make it. Play more games, open all achievements and become #1!

Don’t forget to tell your friends how good you are - share your personal results on Facebook, Twitter or via email.

And remember “Once a winner, always a winner"!!!

## 核心循环

点击/滑动操作 → 消除/得分 → 解锁新关卡或获得道具

## 核心机制

- **核心操作**: 益智解谜单点操作
- **目标系统**: 通过匹配/消除/填充达成关卡目标
- **进度机制**: 关卡递进+星级评价
