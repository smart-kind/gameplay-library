## 玩法规则

Little Galaxy is an interactive sensory app designed for toddlers aged 1–5. Blast off into a magical space adventure where every tap, touch and keystroke creates vibrant planets, shooting stars and cosmic wonders — all set to beautiful celestial melodies. SIX WORLDS TO EXPLORE Cosmic Playground — The classic galaxy experience. Planets, aliens and rainbows fill the sky with every touch. Dino Nebula — Stellar dinosaurs roam the cosmos. T-Rexes, sauropods and volcanoes erupt across a prehistoric sky. Saturn Dreams — Discover life on Saturn's rings. UFOs, fairies and glowing blossoms drift through golden light. Starship Sands — Galactic ships cross the stellar desert. Rockets, satellites and fireballs streak across a war-torn sky. Electric Universe — A world of ions and energy. Atoms, magnets and crystals pulse with electric light. Deep Space — The original dark cosmos. Pure starfields, comets and constellations for the quietest little explorers. REACTIVE SOUNDS Every tap plays a unique musical note tuned to each world's atmosphere — from dreamy pentatonic melodies to prehistoric rumbles and electric pulses. DESIGNED FOR LITTLE HANDS Simple, immediate and endlessly satisfying. No levels, no timers, no rules — just pure cause and effect joy. Perfect for toddlers discovering screens for the first time. SAFE & PRIVATE No ads. No data collected. No accounts. No in-app purchases. No external links during gameplay. Designed with children's safety as the absolute priority. Little Galaxy
