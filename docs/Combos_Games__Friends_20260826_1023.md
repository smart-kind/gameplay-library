# Combos: Games & Friends

- **Type**: Mini Game Collection / Party Game

- **One-liner**: A collection of quick-play mini games designed for casual entertainment

## Gameplay Rules

The app presents a selection of quick-play mini games, each designed for short play sessions of 1-5 minutes. Players choose from the available games on the main menu and dive straight into gameplay.

Each mini game has simple controls - tap, swipe, or drag. The controls are explained briefly before the first play, then players learn by doing. Games range from reaction-based challenges to simple puzzles to physics-based fun.

Many mini games support local multiplayer where multiple players take turns or compete simultaneously on the same device. This makes the collection ideal for social gatherings and family entertainment.

Scores are tracked per game, encouraging players to beat their own records or compete with friends. Some games have progressive difficulty levels within their own mini-campaigns.

## Core Loop

Select mini game -> Play quick session -> See score -> Choose next game

## Core Mechanics

- Quick-Play Design: Each mini game is designed for 1-5 minute sessions, perfect for filling short breaks. Minimal loading time between games.
- Local Multiplayer: Many games support same-device multiplayer, with alternating turns or simultaneous control schemes (split screen, shared screen).
- Score Tracking: Individual high scores for each mini game, encouraging replay and friendly competition.

## Why It Is Fun

The variety and quick-play nature means there is always something new to try. Perfect for social settings where people take turns or compete.

## Retention Drivers

The party and social aspect is the key retention driver - players come back when they have friends to play with. Regular content updates add new mini games. Score chasing motivates solo replay.

## Meta Systems

Score tracking system: high scores per game, total games played statistics, unlockable content based on achievements.

## Implementation Difficulty

Medium - Each mini game needs to be individually designed and balanced. Challenge: creating enough variety to keep the collection fresh.
