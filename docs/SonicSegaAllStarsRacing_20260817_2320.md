## 玩法规则

True to form, Sega is now taking its second attempt at the character-based racing genre (the first being the truly brilliant Sonic R on the aforementioned Sega Saturn, by the way) and it should come as no real surprise to learn that the company has looked towards Nintendo’s insanely successful Mario Kart series for inspiration.

Thankfully, with the obscenely-talented Sumo Digital in the driving seat, Sonic &amp; Sega All-Stars Racing manages to avoid the pitfalls of being a pale imitation and instead succeeds in offering a perfectly viable racing alternative for Sega fans to enjoy.

While Mario Kart is happy to stick to its title franchise for drivers, Sonic &amp; Sega All-Stars Racing cherry-picks famous faces from Sega’s illustrious history.

Naturally, Sonic is present - along with cohort Tails and arch-nemesis Dr Robotnik - but there’s also Billy Hatcher (from the underrated GameCube title Billy Hatcher and the Giant Egg, fact fans), as well as Sega’s long-forgotten mascot Alex Kidd, who was rudely usurped when a certain blue hedgehog came along.

The gameplay is just as pleasing as the selection of drivers available and, again, cues are unashamedly taken from Mario Kart for everything - from the handling of the cars to the selection of weapons on offer.

## 核心循环

选择角色参加竞速 → 使用道具和All-Star技能攻击对手 → 赢得比赛解锁新角色和赛道

## 核心机制

- 角色竞速：汇集Sege多个经典IP角色同场竞技
- All-Star变身技能：每个角色有独特的All-Star变形能力
- 道具系统：比赛中获取和使用道具攻击对手
- 多赛道模式：不同主题的赛道和天气系统

## 为什么好玩

汇集了Sege众多经典角色，从索尼克到Billy Hatcher再到Ulala，角色阵容令人兴奋。All-Star变身系统让比赛充满变数，赛道设计和道具系统也很成熟。

## 粘性来源

收集欲（解锁所有角色和赛道）和社交竞争（与朋友比拼名次）是核心粘性。All-Star技能的华丽效果提供了爽感反馈，让人想不断尝试不同的角色组合。

## Meta 系统

角色和赛道解锁系统，通过比赛获得积分解锁新内容。All-Star技能也有升级路径。

## 实现难度

高 — 3D竞速渲染、物理驾驶模型、道具系统和AI对手是主要技术难点。All-Star变身动画需要高质量的3D建模和动画，多角色平衡性调整也是大工程。
