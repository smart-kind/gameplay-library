# Block Away - Tap Out Puzzle

- **类型**: 休闲 / 益智解谜
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)
- **开发商**: Tripledot Studios
- **首次发布**: 2024
- **一句话描述**: Block Away Puzzle is a challenging and engaging game where players strategically move and rotate blocks to clear the board. The objective is to fit the blocks together in a way that eliminates rows or columns, testing your spatial reasoning and problem-solving skills. With increasing levels of difficulty, each puzzle requires careful planning and foresight to achieve the highest score. Perfect for puzzle enthusiasts looking for a fun and mentally stimulating experience.

## 玩法规则

（基于App Store描述整理）

Block Away Puzzle is a challenging and engaging game where players strategically move and rotate blocks to clear the board. The objective is to fit the blocks together in a way that eliminates rows or columns, testing your spatial reasoning and problem-solving skills. With increasing levels of difficulty, each puzzle requires careful planning and foresight to achieve the highest score. Perfect for puzzle enthusiasts looking for a fun and mentally stimulating experience.

## 核心循环

观察谜题 → 执行操作解题 → 获得成就感/推进进度 → 挑战更复杂谜题

## 为什么好玩

游戏以简单直观的操作门槛吸引了大量休闲玩家，同时又通过精心设计的关卡难度曲线保持了足够的挑战性。"易于上手、难于精通"的设计哲学让不同水平的玩家都能找到乐趣。

## 粘性来源

精心设计的关卡难度曲线让每次过关都有成就感，"再来一局"的心理来自于"差一点就完美通过"的不甘心。多样化的关卡布局和视觉主题保持了长期的新鲜感。

## Meta 系统

- **VIP订阅**：提供去广告和额外每日旋转奖励的订阅服务。
- **关卡解锁**：基础关卡免费，高级内容可能需要付费解锁。
## 实现难度

低。核心玩法为经典的方块匹配/消除逻辑，实现相对简单。主要工作量在于关卡设计和美术资源制作。适合独立开发者或小型团队。
