## 玩法规则

- ► SIZE MATTERS! 

Ever thought how much you’d like to make your favorite ice cream bigger or shrink your team's goal in a soccer game? Or to wave a magic wand and make everything in the world fit just right? Now imagine you have a super hi-tech laser beam that can do just that! That’s what you get in the fun new puzzle-solving game Re-Size-It—the chance to supersize (or supershrink) the object of your choice in order to find the solutions to an endless series of engaging, ingenious physics-based brainteasers
- ► EXPAND YOUR MIND!

• BIG ON LOGIC: What doesn’t fit in this picture? You’ll have to think hard to solve the huge range of unique puzzles in this enthralling physics game where you have to resize all kinds of objects
- A basketball doesn’t fit into the ring—no problem! A shoe is too tight—the ray will fix that
- A picture doesn’t match the frame size – you know what to do! Engage your logical thinking, grab your laser and get blasting!


• BIG ON VARIETY:  With over 200 objects that can be shrunk or expanded and a huge range of locations from single rooms to whole cities, Re-Size-It offers a mind-blowing number of puzzle variations, providing an intense challenge for your logic and your perception
- You might think you know the answer because you’ve seen this object before, but wait a second… Not so easy after all, is it?


• GET OUT THE BIG GUNS! Get thinking and solve puzzle after puzzle to collect dozens of cool-looking, uniquely designed laser guns, marking your progress in this highly original physics game as you scale the giddy heights of re-sizing difficulty

## 核心循环

逻辑 -> 获取资源/分数 -> 升级/解锁新内容

## 核心机制

- 逻辑推理：通过空间思维和逻辑推理来解决逐步复杂的谜题，每关都有唯一解法
- 渐进难度：游戏通过逐步增加复杂度来维持挑战性，从简单规则开始逐渐引入新元素
- 反馈系统：操作后立即获得视觉和数值反馈，强化玩家的行为认知

## 为什么好玩

Re-Size-I‪t‬: Brain Teaser通过将逻辑/解谜的核心体验浓缩到移动端，让玩家随时随地享受策略思考或解谜的乐趣。游戏的简洁设计和明确目标使其上手门槛极低，但深度策略/逻辑层次又保证了长期的可玩性。

## 粘性来源

具体到机制层面：渐进式解锁带来的成就感驱动持续游玩；每局游戏的差异化（随机生成/多人对抗）保证新鲜感；明确的评分/排行榜系统激发竞争欲望；"再来一局"的短局设计降低退出成本。

## Meta 系统

外围成长系统因具体游戏而异。对于策略棋盘类，通常包含Elo评分匹配和赛季排名；对于解谜类，包含关卡进度解锁和每日挑战；对于生存建造类，包含资源积累和基地建设升级。

## 实现难度

中 — 逻辑/解谜游戏的核心规则相对明确，主要难点在于：AI对手的逻辑设计（策略类）、关卡生成的合理性（解谜类）、多人同步的性能优化（生存类）。对于独立开发者来说，核心玩法原型可在2-4周内搭建，但打磨体验和平衡性需要数月迭代。
