# Crazy Penguin Assault

- **类型**: Action/Casual
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)
- **开发商**: PocketGamer reviewed title
- **首次发布**: N/A (delisted)
- **一句话描述**: Action casual game featuring penguins in a physics-based launch and assault gameplay.

## 玩法规则

Players launch penguins as projectiles toward enemy targets and destructible structures. The game uses physics-based mechanics where players set the launch angle and power for each penguin, adjusting for gravity and bounce. The game features destructible environments where hitting structures causes them to crumble and collapse onto enemies. Players have a limited number of penguins per level, requiring strategic allocation of each shot.

The touch controls allow players to drag back on the penguin to set angle and power, similar to a slingshot mechanic, then release to launch the penguin along the calculated trajectory. A trajectory preview line shows the predicted path.

Each level features a different arrangement of enemies, structures, and obstacles. Some enemies are protected by walls or ceilings that must be destroyed first. Special penguin types may be available, such as explosive penguins that detonate on impact, or heavy penguins that can smash through stronger structures. The game displays the remaining penguin count, current score, and star rating targets at the top of the screen.


游戏节奏以关卡为单位推进，每关有独特的结构布局和敌人配置。玩家可以通过多次尝试优化策略，追求更高的星级评价。游戏没有复杂的内购机制，所有内容和道具通过正常游戏进度逐步解锁。

## 核心循环

Aim penguin launcher - Set power and angle - Launch penguin - Watch destruction - Earn points - Progress to next level

## 核心机制

- Slingshot Launch: Players drag back on the penguin to set angle and power for projectiles, with physics determining the flight path, bounce, and impact effects. A trajectory preview helps players plan their shot.
- Destructible Environments: Levels feature breakable structures and obstacles that crumble when hit by penguins, potentially causing chain reactions that multiply damage and score.
- Limited Shots: Players have a set number of penguins per level, requiring strategic allocation and careful aiming. Running out of penguins without clearing all targets means level failure.
- Star Rating System: Performance is rated with 1-3 stars based on score achieved, encouraging replay for better results and mastery of each level.

## 为什么好玩

The combination of satisfying physics-based destruction and the inherently comedic image of penguins as projectiles creates a lighthearted but engaging action-puzzle experience that appeals to casual players. Watching a carefully planned shot trigger a chain reaction of destruction is deeply satisfying.

## 粘性来源

The three-star rating system drives replayability as players try to optimize each level. The variety of level designs and the satisfaction of perfectly destroying complex structures keeps players returning. Special penguin types add variety and strategic depth.

## Meta 系统

Level progression with star ratings. Higher scores unlock new levels and bonus content. Leaderboard competition for high scores. Special penguin types unlocked through achievement milestones.

## 实现难度

Low - Physics engine real-time simulation and large debris particle rendering optimization for smooth performance on mobile devices across different hardware tiers.
