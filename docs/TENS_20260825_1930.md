## 玩法规则

（基于App Store描述整理）

Get ready for the ultimate challenge! Journey through diverse levels, work towards objectives that keep you engaged and entertained as you strategically place dice to make ten. Crate blocks and obstacles won’t make this easy so use special tiles including bombs, and powerful boosters to clear the board.

Use your intellect and efficiency to make combos, unlock new boosters, and beat high scores!  The satisfying feeling of clearing a board makes Tens! the ultimate puzzle adventure to challenge your mind.

Are you prepared to work smarter, not harder and give your IQ a boost? Features: Dynamic Gameplay: Match numbered tiles strategically to reach the sum of ten, each level offers a new twist through objectives.

Diverse Levels: Explore a variety of stages with various challenges and objectives to keep you engaged. Special Tiles & Boosters: Encounter unique tiles such as bombs and utilise useful abilities to enhance your strategy. Exciting challenges: Face off against tricky obstacles like blocker tiles, mastering strategic tile placement.

## 核心循环

匹配相同方块 → 消除获得分数 → 清理棋盘 → 进入下一关

## 核心机制

- **道具辅助系统**：提供撤销、洗牌、磁铁等辅助道具，帮助玩家在卡关时打破僵局，降低难度的同时保持策略深度。

## 为什么好玩

游戏将解谜与叙事巧妙结合，每解开一个谜题都推动故事向前发展，手工艺般的美术风格让探索过程本身成为一种享受。

## 粘性来源

精心设计的关卡难度曲线让每次过关都有成就感，"再来一局"的心理来自于"差一点就完美通过"的不甘心。多样化的关卡布局和视觉主题保持了长期的新鲜感。

## Meta 系统

- **道具系统**：通过游戏内货币购买或关卡奖励获得道具（撤销、洗牌、磁铁等），降低卡关挫败感。
- **关卡进度**：逐步解锁新关卡和机制，难度曲线平缓上升。
## 实现难度

低。核心玩法为经典的方块匹配/消除逻辑，实现相对简单。主要工作量在于关卡设计和美术资源制作。适合独立开发者或小型团队。
