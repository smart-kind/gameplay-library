The official Slugterra mobile RPG is free to play. Collect the slugs from the show, battle Dr. Blakk and the Shadow Clan, and defend the 99 Caverns in a tactical match-3 puzzle RPG. Beginner friendly with a high skill ceiling. Slug It Out 2 is the official Slugterra game. Build your slug team from over 100 creatures straight from the animated series, master elemental battle strategy, and fight through a full story campaign set in the Slugterra universe fans already know. Collect the Slugs from the Show Over 100 official Slugterra slugs spanning Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Energy, and Psychic elements, each with distinct abilities, upgrade paths, and evolution stages. Level up your favorites, unlock legendary skills, and build teams around the slugs you know from the series. Go aggressive, defensive, elemental, or combo-based. Adapt your roster as the challenge grows. Tactical Match-3 Slug Battles Every puzzle move charges your slug team and fuels signature attacks. Chain tiles for bonus energy, set up multi-hit combos, and time abilities to counter enemy mechanics. Easy to learn for new players, deep enough for veteran Slugterra trainers who want to master every element and synergy combination. Defend Slugterra Across the 99 Caverns Explore the full underground world of Slugterra across dozens of story missions and increasingly dangerous zones. Face Dr. Blakk, the Shadow Clan, and other iconic villains from the animated series. Earn progression rewards and uncover hidden content
