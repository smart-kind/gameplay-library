## 玩法规则

Block Away Puzzle is a challenging and engaging game where players strategically move and rotate blocks to clear the board. The objective is to fit the blocks together in a way that eliminates rows or columns, testing your spatial reasoning and problem-solving skills. With increasing levels of difficulty, each puzzle requires careful planning and foresight to achieve the highest score. Perfect for puzzle enthusiasts looking for a fun and mentally stimulating experience.
