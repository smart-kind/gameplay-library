## 玩法规则

Brick Game - Retro Games is the compilation of the best games from the most popular console of the 1990s. Do you feel tired of with complicated and difficult games? Did you miss the favorite classic games? Let's play this game to feel nostalgic!

Game features:
• 19 games in 1
• Many level and speed
• 11 different classic themes
• 8 bits sound
• Share high score with your friends, family via social apps
• Submit score to leaderboard

Game list:
A - Brick Puzzle Classic:
Move and rotate the falling blocks. Lines are cleared when they are filled with blocks and have no empty spaces

B - Tank Classic:
Move tank and shoot bullet to kill enemy. The speed and intelligence of the enemy will increase after each level

C - Racing Classic:
Move racer left right to avoid enemy, the speed will be increase after each level

## 核心循环

单点操作 → 得分/前进 → 挑战更高分或新关卡

## 核心机制

- **核心操作**: 点击发射弹球消除方块
- **目标系统**: 通过匹配/消除/填充达成关卡目标
- **进度机制**: 关卡解锁+社交挑战

## 为什么好玩
