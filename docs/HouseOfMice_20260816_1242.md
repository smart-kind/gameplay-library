# House of Mice

- **类型**: Simulation/Casual
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)
- **开发商**: PocketGamer reviewed title
- **首次发布**: N/A (delisted)
- **一句话描述**: Simulation casual game centered around building and managing a mouse household.

## 玩法规则

Players manage a household or community of mice, building rooms, collecting resources, and caring for mouse characters. The game features a virtual dollhouse-style interface where players construct and decorate rooms for their mouse residents. Players collect food and materials by completing mini-tasks or adventures, then use these resources to expand the mouse house with new rooms, furniture, and decorations.

Mouse characters have individual needs and personalities requiring attention and care. Each mouse has preferences for room types, food, and activities that must be satisfied to keep them happy and productive.

Touch controls tap to interact with mice, drag to place furniture, and swipe to navigate between rooms of the mouse house. The game shows a cross-section view of the mouse house with all rooms visible.


游戏以日常节奏运行，玩家每次登录可以收集资源、照顾老鼠居民并完成日常任务。游戏没有强制的时间压力，玩家可以按照自己的节奏慢慢建设和装饰。游戏的社交元素体现在可以访问其他玩家的鼠标屋获得灵感。

## 核心循环

Collect resources - Build and expand rooms - Care for mouse residents - Complete tasks - Unlock new items - Expand mouse community

## 核心机制

- Room Building and Decoration: Players construct rooms for their mice, choosing from various furniture and decoration options to customize each space with different themes and styles.
- Resource Collection: Food, materials, and coins are collected through mini-games, timed collection from mouse activities, or completing daily tasks and quests.
- Character Care: Individual mouse characters have happiness, hunger, and comfort meters that need regular attention and interaction through feeding, playing, and decorating.
- Collection System: Players collect unique furniture sets, mouse outfits, and room themes to complete their mouse house, with each collection offering visual variety and satisfaction.

## 为什么好玩

The charm of managing a tiny mouse household with cute characters and creative room decoration creates a cozy, relaxing gameplay experience. The gradual expansion from a small mouse hole to a grand mouse mansion is deeply satisfying.

## 粘性来源

The collection aspect of furniture sets, room themes, and mouse characters combined with the steady progression of building a larger mouse house creates long-term engagement. The cute aesthetic and low-pressure gameplay appeal to casual players seeking a relaxing experience.

## Meta 系统

Room expansion and decoration collection. Mouse character collection with unique traits and preferences. Daily tasks and seasonal events for bonus items and limited-time decorations.

## 实现难度

Low - Rendering of many small objects and interaction detection for furniture and decorations in each room, plus mouse AI behavior simulation for individual character needs and activities.
