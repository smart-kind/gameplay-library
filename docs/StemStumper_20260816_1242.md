# Stem Stumper

- **类型**: Puzzle/Word
- **平台**: Mobile (iOS)
- **开发商**: PocketGamer reviewed title
- **首次发布**: N/A (delisted)
- **一句话描述**: Word puzzle game focused on word stems, roots, and etymology challenges.

## 玩法规则

Players solve word puzzles based on word stems, roots, and etymology. Each puzzle presents players with word fragments or stems, and they must identify complete words that share those roots or form new words by combining stems. The game progressively introduces more complex linguistic challenges, from simple prefix and suffix combinations to obscure etymological connections.

Players earn points based on word complexity and speed of solving, with timed challenges and daily puzzles adding variety. Hints are available through word origin clues that teach players about Latin, Greek, and Germanic language roots.

Touch controls let players drag letter tiles to form words or tap from a selection of available stems to build valid words. A timer shows remaining time for timed puzzles, and a score counter tracks points earned.


游戏以每日挑战为核心，同时提供无限的常规谜题供玩家随时游玩。每日谜题有特定的主题或词源焦点，鼓励玩家每天回来尝试新的语言挑战。游戏的难度逐步递增，从常见词汇到专业术语都有覆盖。

## 核心循环

Analyze word stem - Form valid words - Score based on complexity - Unlock harder puzzles - Learn etymology - Improve vocabulary

## 核心机制

- Stem-Based Word Formation: Players are given word stems including prefixes, roots, and suffixes, and must form valid words by combining them in correct ways, testing both vocabulary and linguistic understanding.
- Etymology Hints: Hints are provided through word origin clues such as Latin, Greek, and Germanic roots, teaching players about language history while helping them solve puzzles.
- Difficulty Scaling: Puzzles range from common English words to specialized vocabulary, with difficulty increasing as players progress through levels and unlock more advanced word categories.
- Daily Challenge: A new puzzle each day with a specific theme or linguistic focus, encouraging daily play and habit formation through consistent fresh content.

## 为什么好玩

The educational aspect combined with genuine puzzle challenge makes this game both entertaining and intellectually rewarding. Learning about word origins while solving puzzles creates a satisfying moment of understanding with each solution found.

## 粘性来源

The daily puzzle feature and the desire to improve vocabulary scores create habitual engagement. The educational value means players feel productive while playing, reducing guilt about screen time and encouraging regular play sessions.

## Meta 系统

Vocabulary tracking with stats on words learned. Daily streak system rewarding consecutive play. Achievement badges for etymology milestones and vocabulary range expansion across different word categories.
