- **首次发布**: 2025

## 玩法规则

Endless Fun in One App! 
Welcome to TopTop — the ultimate social gaming app where fun meets friendship!
Packed with addictive multiplayer games and exciting ways to connect, TopTop is your go-to place for nonstop entertainment and real-time social interaction.
Play popular games like:
Who’s the Undercover,  Toppy Park,  ONO,  IQ Games, and more!
Whether you're killing time or looking to bond with friends old and new, TopTop brings people together through games — and we’re always adding more!

Toppy Park
Team up for chaos and cooperation in this 2–8 player multiplayer puzzle game! In Toppy Park, teamwork is the only way to win. Work together to solve tricky puzzles and collect all the keys to advance.
Play anytime, anywhere — online or locally — and get ready for laughter, surprises, and unforgettable team moments

Who’s the Undercover?
A game of wits, bluffing, and hilarious deception! Gather your friends online or offline, take on roles as Innocents, the sneaky Undercover, or the clueless Mr. White.  Describe your secret word without giving it away. Can you spot the liar before it’s too late? Enjoy thousands of FREE word packs & hilarious themes! Supports 4-8 player matches, perfect for both online squad battles and offline parties

Make New Friends
TopTop makes it easy to meet new people while playing. Jump into live PK matches, challenge real-time opponents, and connect with players who share your interests.
Chat, add friends, and invite them for another round anytime!

Hang Together in Groups
TopTop isn’t just for gaming — it’s a social platform, too!
Join voice rooms and chat with people from around the world. Whether you're into music, games, sports, or just good vibes, there's a space for you. No need to show your face — just be yourself, talk freely, and build real connections in a relaxed, fun space

## 核心循环

单点操作 → 得分/前进 → 挑战更高分或新关卡

## 核心机制

- **核心操作**: 社交互动+小游戏合集
- **目标系统**: 通过匹配/消除/填充达成关卡目标
- **进度机制**: 关卡解锁+社交挑战

## 为什么好玩

社交+游戏的双重乐趣，适合和朋友一起消磨时间

## 粘性来源

社交归属感+游戏多样性+每日更新内容

## Meta 系统

- 好友列表、群组聊天、语音房间
- 玩家在游戏外建立社交关系
- 对核心玩法的影响：社交关系链促进回流

## 实现难度

低 — 规则简单清晰，最大难点是关卡多样性和视觉反馈打磨
